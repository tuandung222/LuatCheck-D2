### v3 results (backup / pre-planned D2 result)

- **Runs:** `evaluation/runs/d2-gpu-main-hcmut-v3` and `evaluation/runs/d2-gpu-main-labour-v3`, 545/545 trials each, on server 82 (GPU 2 / GPU 3), 16 workers.
- **Errors:** 5 execution errors (`model_output_error`, runaway `thought`), counted as failures.
- **Judge:** DeepSeek Flash, about USD 1.3. 2 + 5 packets stayed unknown and are counted as failures.
- **Verification:** strict success was recomputed independently and matched in all 10 cells.

| Strict success % [95% CI] | HCMUT | Labour |
|---|---|---|
| OFF | 18.0 [11–26] | 14.7 [7–23] |
| L1 | 12.0 [7–18] | 14.7 [7–23] |
| L1+rerank | 14.0 [8–21] | 18.7 [11–27] |
| closed-book baseline | 6.0 | 14.0 |
| oracle baseline (gold given) | 28.9 | 44.4 |

Paired results:
- **L1 vs OFF:** strict success shows no significant change (−6.0 [−13.3, +0.7] and 0.0); behaviour match falls by 21–23 pp.
- **L1+rerank vs L1:** R@5 +15.6 pp [+3.0, +28.9] on labour.

Main failure modes (`docs/reviews/v3_failure_analysis_2026-10-24.md`):
- clarify is used as a missing-evidence exit;
- model-written queries retrieve worse than the raw question (first-query R@3 0.73/0.63 vs 0.97/0.98);
- L1 repair feedback pushes the 2B model to refuse;
- most L1 rejections are quote mismatches (172 of 237);
- even with gold evidence the 2B model succeeds on only 29–44 %.
