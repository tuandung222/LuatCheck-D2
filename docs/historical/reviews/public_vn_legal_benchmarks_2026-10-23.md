# Public Vietnamese legal datasets and benchmarks: can LuatCheck use one?

Status: desk research with partial hands-on inspection. Every candidate below was opened (dataset card, file tree, and in most cases real rows) on 2026-10-23. Facts that come only from a paper abstract or a web page are marked "(paper)". Numbers that I computed myself from downloaded files are marked "(own count)". Nothing here is a human or legal-expert review.

Scratch files (not in the repo): `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-admin-Desktop-Recents-LuatCheck-D2/3584b6c4-97bd-4d37-8025-11864587c887/scratchpad/` (subfolders `vlb`, `vle`, `zalo`, `vlsp`, `vbpl`, `alq`). Downloads were small (largest file 55 MB; nothing above 200 MB; the 538 MB reranker-train file and the multi-GB vbpl content files were not fetched).

## 1. Bottom line

1. No public dataset is a credible drop-in external benchmark for the whole LuatCheck system (agent loop over our 10-document labour corpus, which contains 2025 instruments). The public resources are either closed-book multiple choice (VLegal-Bench), retrieval-only (Zalo, DRILL), built on older or different corpora (all of them), or not downloadable (ALQAC, VLQA queries, ViHERMES, ViLegalNLI).
2. The closest topical match is ViLegalExperts (`DatNguyenAIML/ViLegalExperts`): real citizen questions with an expert-verified article reference. Its test split has 247 items whose reference is Bộ luật Lao động 2019 (BLLĐ 2019), 205 of them with an article number (own count). Those 205 map one-to-one onto our `BLLD2019_Dieu_<n>` records. It is usable as a secondary, retrieval-only, labelled-external slice for D2. It is not usable for answer-correctness claims (reference answers are long web-article prose) and the HF upload has missing context files (section 3.1).
3. Zalo Legal Text Retrieval 2021 (`GreenNode/zalo-ai-legal-text-retrieval-vn`) and DRILL / VLQA (VLSP 2025) are the established retrieval benchmarks. They fit D3 (domain-general, multi-domain retrieval check of the reranker and BM25 stack) rather than D2. They contain almost no labour law (Zalo: 14 of 793 gold rows, own count).
4. The most valuable thing to borrow for our own v2 is methodology and corpus, not questions: the hard-case taxonomy of arXiv 2609.27376, the amendment-tracing / cross-document patterns of ViHERMES, the Bloom-level and temporal-validity design of VLegal-Bench, and the vbpl.vn corpus with effect status and an amendment graph (`th1nhng0/vietnamese-legal-documents`, CC BY 4.0), which contains all ten of our v1 labour instruments.
5. Contamination and currency risks apply to everything: the sources are public web or competition data (likely in LLM training data), and the Zalo (2021), DRILL (corpus up to 2024) and ViLegalExperts (test items up to 2023) corpora predate the 2025 instruments in `labour_vi_v1`.

## 3. Detailed notes

### 3.1 ViLegalExperts (`DatNguyenAIML/ViLegalExperts`)

- Owner and paper: Dat Tien Nguyen, Nghia Hieu Nguyen, Anh Thi-Hoang Nguyen, Dung Ha Nguyen, Kiet Van Nguyen, Ngan Luu-Thuy Nguyen (UIT, VNU-HCM). arXiv 2609.39189 v1, 2026-09-30. No venue given. HF card has only a citation block; created and last updated 2026-10-06; 1,971 downloads; 1 like; not gated.
- Files (checked): `QA_single_context/{train_data 443 MB, dev_data 55 MB, test_data 56 MB, train_3k_data 8 MB}.json`, `structured-single-hop-IR_single_context/{train 216 MB, dev 27 MB, test 27 MB, train_3k 557 MB}.json`, and a `contexts/` folder of `context_<id>.json` files. Total repo storage about 1.38 GB. I downloaded only `QA_single_context/test_data.json` (55.6 MB) and `structured-single-hop-IR_single_context/test_data.json` (27.3 MB).
- Schema (own check): each item has `question`, `answer`, `link`, `contexts` (exactly one context per item in the test file, own count), `most_common_doc_type`. A context has `document` (e.g. "bộ luật lao động 2019"), `Số hiệu:`, `Loại văn bản:`, `Ngày ban hành:`, `Tình trạng:`, `link` (a thuvienphapluat.vn document URL), `content` (a pointer such as `context_129823.json`), and for 8,542 of 17,821 items an `điều` field and for 3,259 a `khoản` field.
- Construction (paper): questions and professional answers were collected from public online legal consultation platforms; legal documents were collected from official sources; experts checked whether the provisions cited in the answers are valid and relevant, and incorrect references were dropped. The paper gives no annotator count, no agreement figure, no limitations section, and no statement on document versions or superseded laws. The `link` fields I inspected point to thuvienphapluat.vn news-style articles, so the "answers" are web-article text (often a quoted article followed by a short explanation), not short gold answers.
- Size inconsistencies in the paper: abstract "over 172K questions"; Table 1 says 177,158 samples; section 3.2 says "117,158 QA instances" and 87,695 retrieval instances. Treat sizes as approximate.
- Labour-law coverage (own count, test split only): 247 of 17,821 items have "bộ luật lao động 2019" as the context document (second most frequent document after Bộ luật Hình sự 2015 with 250). 205 of the 247 carry an article number. They cover 95 distinct articles of the 220 articles in BLLĐ 2019. Dev and train splits would add more but I did not count them.
- Samples (paraphrased): "If a worker takes unpaid leave that overlaps with a public holiday, is the holiday paid?" and "Does a pregnant worker have the right to terminate the contract?" (gold: BLLĐ 2019 Điều 138 khoản 1). The second question names no article.
- Data quality issues found:
  1. The test items reference 4,912 distinct context files. In a random sample of 60 of them, only 1 could be fetched from the HF repo (HTTP "Entry not found" for the others, e.g. the BLLĐ 2019 context `context_129823.json`). The folder listing returned 9,996 files with ids up to 108,993, while referenced ids go up to 306,314. Either my listing was capped or the upload is incomplete; cause not determined. Consequence: the shipped corpus cannot be used as is. For labour items we would use our own BLLĐ 2019 text instead.
  2. The `Tình trạng:` (legal status) field is the placeholder "Đã biết" in 15,900 of 17,821 test contexts (89%), and `Ngày hiệu lực:` is also a placeholder in the example I opened. Currency of the cited law cannot be read from the metadata.
  3. Document issue dates in the test split run from 2012 to 2023 (own count); there are no 2024 or later instruments. Items are about 2023-era law.
- Difficulty on our corpus (own count, not the system retriever): I ran a plain BM25 (syllable unigrams, question text only, same recipe as the benchmark audit) over `data/corpus/labour_vi_v1.json` (458 records, 10 documents) for the 205 article-level items, with gold `BLLD2019_Dieu_<n>`. Gold at rank 1 for 44.4%, in the top 5 for 71.7%, in the top 10 for 80.0%, median rank 2. Only 1 of 205 questions names an article or the code. For comparison, the audit found 78% rank-1 on `labour_vi_v1`. So these questions are noticeably harder to retrieve than our v1 items. Caveat: the gold set may be incomplete (a lawyer answer may rely on a decree that is not in our corpus), so some failures may be gold gaps, not retrieval failures.
- Fit: retrieval-only secondary slice. Mapping to our schema is easy for the 205 items (`ground_truth_article_ids: ["BLLD2019_Dieu_<n>"]`, `expected_behavior: answer`). Not suitable for answer correctness or refusal tests. Contamination: TVPL pages are public web text, likely in LLM training data. Licensing: the dataset has no stated license and the texts are scraped from a commercial legal portal, so check before redistributing anything; keeping only ids and our own text in a task file avoids redistributing their text.

### 3.2 VLegal-Bench (`CMC-OPENAI/VLegal-Bench`; `Anonymous-User-Legal-Bench/VietLegal`)

- Owner and paper: Nguyen Tien Dong, Minh-Anh Nguyen, Thanh Dat Hoang, Nguyen Tuan Ngoc, Dao Xuan Quang Minh, Phan Phi Hai, Nguyen Thi Ngoc Anh, Dang Van Tu, Binh Vu (CMC OpenAI; VinUniversity). arXiv 2512.14554, v1 2025-12-16, current v6 2026-10-19. A listing "Benchmarking Vietnamese Legal Knowledge of Large Language Models" appears in the ICML 2026 virtual site under the same benchmark (from search result; I did not open that page). The v6 paper's checklist lists the anonymous repository as the review copy of the same benchmark; the two HF repos have the same 22-task layout but slightly different file sizes and counts (e.g. task 1.1 file 1,196,131 vs 1,176,401 bytes), so they are two versions, not byte-identical.
- Construction (paper, v6): about 55,000 currently effective central legal documents processed by HTML parsing and OCR; experts annotated; senior lawyer supervision; blind cross-verification in batches of 100; initial agreement 92.39% with Cohen's kappa 0.89. The paper says contamination screening flagged 1.8% of 1,000 sampled instances. Limitations noted by the authors: recency (2024 to 2025 instruments), sub-article granularity, metric-expert correlation 0.44 to 0.66.
- Content (own check of 19 files): task folders 1.1 to 5.4; items are mostly `{instruction, question, answers (A to D), ground_truth (letter)}`. Task 3.1 asks to pick the correct article or clause among four options (the distractors are fabricated article numbers); 3.3 gives the article text and asks a scenario question; 3.4 is conflict detection between two norms; 3.5 is penalty estimation; 4.x are generation tasks. The paper says an agentic RAG evaluation on 3.1 to 3.4 uses a "Legal Corpus Database", but that corpus is not in the HF repository (file tree checked).
- Labour coverage (own regex count): 38 items name Bộ luật Lao động; about 610 mention "lao động" anywhere (e.g. as part of a general question); this is a loose upper bound, not a labour-law subset.
- License: `LICENSE.txt` in the repo says CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, attribution "Dataset created by CMC-OpenAI", and forbids derivative works. Converting items into our task schema is arguably a derivative; do not redistribute converted items without asking the owners (technicaldept@cmcai.vn is the contact in the license).
- Fit: 1. Closed-book multiple choice does not exercise retrieval or citation. Useful as related work and for design ideas (cognitive levels; conflict / temporal tasks).

### 3.3 Zalo AI Legal Text Retrieval 2021 (`GreenNode/zalo-ai-legal-text-retrieval-vn`)

- Owner: Zalo AI Challenge 2021 data, re-hosted for MTEB by GreenNode (task name `ZacLegalTextRetrieval`). Card: annotations "human-annotated", MIT license, Vietnamese, 5.6K downloads, 6 likes, not gated. Card cites GN-TRVN (Springer 2026) and MMTEB (arXiv 2502.13595) for the MTEB re-hosting; I did not find an original Zalo paper.
- Size (own count): corpus 61,425 articles from 3,271 laws; test queries 818; test qrels 793 rows covering 788 unique queries (5 queries have more than one gold article); train qrels 2,505 rows; 285 distinct gold laws.
- Concentration: ND 100/2019/NĐ-CP (traffic fines) is the gold law for 206 of 793 rows (26%). The vbpl.vn portal lists ND 100/2019 as partially expired ("Hết hiệu lực một phần") and ND 168/2024/NĐ-CP as in force, so part of the gold is superseded.
- Labour: 13 gold rows from TT-BLĐTBXH circulars and 1 row from 12/2012/QH13 (the 2012 Trade Union Law, which vbpl.vn lists as fully expired on 2025-07-01). BLLĐ 2019 and BLLĐ 2012 are not in the corpus (own count).
- Samples (paraphrased): "What is the fine for making a U-turn on an expressway?"; "What are the funding sources of the Vietnam Register?".
- Relevance to us: our reranker `AITeamVN/Vietnamese_Reranker` reports Accuracy@1 0.7944 and MRR@10 0.8672 on "entire training dataset of Legal Zalo 2021" and states it was not trained on it (model card, self-reported, 1.1M training triplets of undisclosed composition). A retrieval-only run over this corpus would give an independent check of those claims for our BM25 + reranker stack, but it tests the reranker in a traffic-law-dominated, 2021 setting and does not test the agent.
- Fit: 2 for D3 as a retrieval component check; 0 for labour claims.

### 3.4 DRILL (VLSP 2025) and VLQA

- Papers: VLQA (arXiv 2507.19995, 2025-07): 3,129 question-article-answer triplets, split 7:1:2; 59,636 articles from 2,162 legal documents; 27 domains; questions drawn from about 430,000 lay questions on legal advice sites; annotated by five senior law students with an experienced legal expert as reviewer, each pair annotated independently by two annotators; relevant articles given as Law_id + Article_id; average 1.33 relevant articles, maximum 9. Baselines (paper): BM25 zero-shot Recall@2 0.3835, BGE-m3 0.5442, BGE-rerank two-stage 0.5845. Labour is the "Labor and Wages" domain (2,440 articles in the paper's Table 5). The page says the dataset "will be publicly released soon" and gives no license (the CC BY-NC-SA on the arXiv page is for the article).
- DRILL overview (ACL Anthology 2025.vlsp-1.16, pp. 113 to 119; I read the PDF): train 2,190, public test 312, private test 627 questions; 1.34 relevant articles per query on average; 5 coarse domains (Economics and Finance, State Management and Law, Society Culture Education, Infrastructure and Development, Science and Technology); hosted on Codabench; over 50 teams.
- What is on HF (own check): `WeDev-VLSP/legal-corpus` and `jasong03/legal-drill-vlsp` have the same corpus, 2,157 law rows, nested article lists (`law_id`, `aid`, `content_Article`), 33 MB, no license in the card. Total articles 59,636 (matches the paper). It contains `45/2019/QH14` with 220 articles (same count as our `BLLD2019` records), `135/2020/NĐ-CP`, `145/2020/NĐ-CP`, `10/2012/QH13`, 48 TT-BLĐTBXH laws, and none of our 2025 instruments (143/2024, 293/2025, 318/2025, 219/2025, 274/2025, 26/2025 checked absent). The latest issue year among law ids is 2024 (13 laws).
- Not found: the question files (public test and private test labels) on HF. `VietGPT-AI/vlsp-legal-dataset-public-test-split` is gated (manual). `hoailebads/VLSP-Legal-Reranker-Train` (Apache-2.0, 9,994 training rows of which 1,987 are "vlsp", 2,477 "zalo", 5,530 synthetic) is derived training data, not a test set. Official question access (VLSP site / Codabench registration) not verified.
- Fit: 2 for D3 only if the official questions can be obtained; otherwise the corpus mirror is a candidate multi-domain corpus (with the labour documents in it) but without reviewed questions. Do not reuse its results as evidence for other domains (AGENTS.md rule).

### 3.5 ALQAC (2021 to 2026)

- Organizer: NguyenLab, associated with KSE (2025: 5th edition, 6 to 8 Nov 2025, Dalat; ALQAC 2026 scheduled 11 to 14 Nov 2026, Kanazawa, a different task: case outcome prediction with evidence retrieval on court judgments). I read the 2024, 2025 and 2026 competition pages.
- 2024 and 2025 task format (site): Task 1 retrieve the relevant articles (macro F2); Task 2 answer the question (true/false, multiple choice A to D, free text judged by humans); gold `relevant_articles` as `law_id` + `article_id`; "manually annotated" data on "well-known statute laws" (examples: Cinema Law 05/2022/QH15, Marriage and Family Law 52/2014/QH13). Dataset sizes, domain list and dataset license are not stated on the pages; no direct download link; the 2025 rules prohibit closed proprietary models and allow only open-weight models under 10B for participants.
- HF: `nguyenlab/ALQAC` (gated, request required; 223 downloads, 5 likes) has folders `ALQAC_2023`, `ALQAC_2024`, `ALQAC_2025`, `additional_data` (Zalo and 2022 training data per the card snippet). Contents not inspected (access denied). Third-party `VietTung04/alqac_2025` (0 likes, no license, provenance not documented) has 729 train-style items with the article text as `context` and no article ids; domains in the sampled rows are family law, residence, administrative sanctions. Another third-party user (`nguyenkhanh87/ViLegalQA-Synthetic-Curation`, 10,095 synthetic items, 600 with five-expert calibration labels) deliberately ships only ids, not text, and points to the gated official repo for the context.
- Domain mix: the arXiv 2609.27376 paper says it took 80 labour-law records from ALQAC 2021; I could not verify this independently.
- Fit: 1. The format (statute article gold + answer types) is close to ours, but the data is gated, mostly non-labour, and competition rules apply.

### 3.6 Labour-law paper arXiv 2609.27376 (closest topical match, no dataset found)

- "Cross-Lingual Legal QA for Vietnamese Labour Law: Retrieval, Translation, and Verifier-Guided Correction", Nguyen Minh Chi, Mo El-Haj, Nguyen Ha Thanh, Dawn Knight, Paul Rayson; submitted 2026-09-23; NLLP 2026 at EMNLP 2026.
- Suite (read from the HTML version): 231 VI-EN QA pairs built from VLegal-Bench (156 Labour-Code records from seven tasks) and ALQAC 2021 (80 records), 5 exact duplicates removed; a community collection "VietLegal" with 878 questions was screened out because citation coverage was incomplete. English was machine-translated with GPT-4o-mini and only spot-checked. Corpus: 953 articles from 20 instrument versions, including the 2019 Code, its consolidated text dated 2026-02-12 (No. 18/VBHN-VPQH) and implementing decrees. Hard-case tags (by one rater, no agreement): exception-conditional 41, cross-article definitions 12, numeric thresholds 11, temporal thresholds 6, synthesis of non-adjacent articles 5. 88 records (38%) need three or more gold articles. Gold citations are bare article numbers (ambiguous across instruments). Human evaluation: 27 questions, two graduate students.
- Findings relevant to us (paper): dense retrieval R@5 0.358 vs learned-sparse 0.032 for English queries over the Vietnamese corpus; verifier-guided correction improved citation preservation by 0.022 to 0.034 with no reliable gains elsewhere. The paper's own limitations say gold may be outdated because 2025 amendments were not fully reviewed.
- Release: no data or code URL on the abstract page; license of the data not stated. Status: unverified availability. Worth contacting the authors if we want the 231 pairs; otherwise cite as related work and borrow the hard-case tags.
- Fit: 1 (cite; borrow tags). It is also the closest peer to our system's design (retrieval plus a verifier on labour law), so it belongs in related work.

### 3.7 vbpl.vn corpus (`th1nhng0/vietnamese-legal-documents`) as a resource for v2

- Curator: Thinh Ngo; source vbpl.vn (Ministry of Justice portal); CC BY 4.0; 27.3K downloads, 49 likes; refreshed August 2026 (2026 portal refresh noted on the card). Configs: `metadata` (171,556 rows, 17 fields incl. `tinh_trang_hieu_luc`, `ngay_het_hieu_luc`), `content` (170,824 HTML bodies, 785 MB), `relationships` (1,033,255 directed edges such as amendments, citations, repeals), plus legacy configs (518K documents, 3.5 GB).
- Check against our 10 documents (own count from the 15 MB metadata file): present are 45/2019/QH14 (two rows with different status values: in force and partially expired, a duplicate that needs care), 135/2020/NĐ-CP (partially expired), 143/2024/NĐ-CP, 293/2025/NĐ-CP, 318/2025/NĐ-CP, 219/2025/NĐ-CP, 274/2025/NĐ-CP, 74/2025/QH15 (Employment Law 2025), 50/2024/QH15 (Trade Union Law 2024, partially expired per the portal), and several `26/2025/TT-*` rows for different ministries (the labour ministry circular needs a specific lookup). Latest issue dates reach 2026.
- Use: provenance and currency checks for our corpus (is each instrument in force, has it been amended), and the relationship graph as raw material for temporal and amendment-tracing questions in v2 (`as_of_date` field). Not a benchmark; no questions. The portal's status field is the portal's classification, not legal advice.

### 3.8 ViHERMES and ViRHE4QA (regulation QA, secondary)

- ViHERMES (`ura-hcmut/ViHERMES`, arXiv 2602.07361, ACIIDS 2026, HCMUT URA lab): multi-hop QA over Vietnamese healthcare regulations built by a controlled LLM generation pipeline (semantic clustering plus graph mining) with patterns amendment tracing, cross-document comparison and procedural synthesis, plus a graph-aware retrieval system. HF repo gated, so size and contents were not verified; the paper page does not state size. Methodologically the closest to our v2 plan (multi-hop across amended and cross-referenced regulations), and from the same university as `hcmut_v1`. Gold is LLM-generated, so correctness is not expert-verified (paper).
- ViRHE4QA / R2GQA (arXiv 2409.02840): 9,758 QA pairs on higher-education training regulations (extractive and abstractive) (paper, via search snippet). The HF repo `TaiDoan123/UIT-R2GQA` is a derived retrieval-training format (pair-class and triplet configs; 15,612 / 1,952 / 1,952 pair rows), not the original QA set. Relevant to `hcmut_v1`, not to labour law. Original data location not verified.

## 7. Method and limits

- Search: Hugging Face Hub search for 30+ terms (vietnamese legal, vietnamese law, zalo legal, ALQAC, VLSP legal, VietLegal, legal-vi, VLQA, DRILL, ViLegal, lao dong, MLQA-TSR, VMLU, ViLLM-Eval, ViHERMES, R2GQA, BKAI legal, SoICT legal, owner scopes bkai-foundation-models, GreenNode, AITeamVN), plus web searches for ALQAC, VLSP 2025, VLegal-Bench, ViLegalExpert, VLQA, DRILL, labour-law RAG. Searches with Vietnamese diacritics (`pháp luật`, `luật`) returned nothing from the HF search endpoint, so coverage relies on English and ASCII terms. I did not check Semantic Scholar or GitHub search directly. The orgs `vinai`, `uitnlp`, `ZaloAI`, `phamson02`'s peers and `truro7` were not searched individually (only through keyword search across all datasets), so datasets published by them under non-obvious names may be missing.
- Inspected by reading rows or files: ViLegalExperts (test QA and IR files), VLegal-Bench (19 task files), Zalo (queries, qrels, corpus parquet), DRILL corpus mirror (parquet), ALQAC 2025 re-upload (3 parquet files), vbpl.vn metadata (parquet), NamSyntax, EvalRAGData.
- Papers: abstracts or full text read via arXiv / ACL pages for VLegal-Bench (v6 HTML, first 100K characters), VLQA (first 100K characters), 2609.27376 (HTML), ViLegalExpert (PDF, full), DRILL (PDF, full), ViHERMES and ViLegalNLI (abstract page only), ALQAC competition sites 2024 to 2026.
- Not done: no model was run; the ALQAC and ViHERMES gated repos were not opened; dev and train splits of ViLegalExperts were not counted; VLegal-Bench paper's appendices were not read (only the first 100K of 202K characters); no legal-expert review of any item; the labour-law counts for VLegal-Bench come from a loose regex.
- Dates: arXiv ids 2609.xxxxx and 2605.xxxxx are from pages fetched on 2026-10-23 and are cited as returned by the tools.
