\textbf{Executive Summary.} LuatCheck is a local, single-agent regulatory assistant over HCMUT academic regulations and Vietnamese labour law, driven by a 4B controller (2B option) via \texttt{llama.cpp}. It combines hybrid retrieval, cross-encoder reranking, pre-model regex guardrails, and a deterministic Python citation verifier ($<2$\,ms, zero LLM calls) with bounded repair. The D2 evaluation establishes a reproducible baseline across 1{,}090 trials per controller on two 50-question benchmarks (validated by a 60-item blind human audit, $\kappa = 0.80\text{--}0.93$), ahead of broader unseen and adversarial experiments in D3. Scaling from 2B to 4B raises strict success from 28.0--28.7\% to 56.7--60.7\% ($+28.7$ to $+32.0$\,pp); the L1 citation verifier cuts unverified citations from $25.6\text{--}28.0\%$ to $0.0\%$ and lifts audited strict success from 55.3\% to 60.7--64.0\% (HCMUT) and 51.3\% to 56.7\% (labour law).\par\vspace{2.5pt}\hrule\vspace{2.5pt}
