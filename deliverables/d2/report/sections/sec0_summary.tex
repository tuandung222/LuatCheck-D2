\textbf{Executive Summary.} LuatCheck is a local, single-agent regulatory assistant over HCMUT academic regulations and Vietnamese labour law, driven by a 4B controller (with a lighter 2B option) served via \texttt{llama.cpp}. It combines hybrid retrieval and cross-encoder reranking, pre-model regex guardrails, and a deterministic Python citation verifier ($<2$\,ms, zero LLM calls) with bounded repair. The D2 evaluation establishes a reproducible baseline across 1{,}090 recorded trials per controller on two 50-question Vietnamese benchmarks (validated against a 60-item blind human audit, $\kappa = 0.80\text{--}0.93$), ahead of broader unseen and adversarial experiments in D3. Scaling the controller from 2B to 4B increases strict success from 28.0--28.7\% to 56.7--60.7\% ($+28.7$ to $+32.0$\,pp); enabling the rule-based citation verifier eliminates unverified citations ($25.6\text{--}28.0\% \to 0.0\%$) and raises citation-audited strict success from 55.3\% to 60.7--64.0\% on HCMUT and from 51.3\% to 56.7\% on labour law.}}
