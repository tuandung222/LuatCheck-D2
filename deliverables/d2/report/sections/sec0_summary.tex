\textbf{Summary.} LuatCheck answers regulatory questions strictly from a local document corpus, cites the supporting provisions, verifies each citation deterministically against the source text in Python, and refuses or requests clarification when the corpus does not contain sufficient evidence. It is a single bounded agent whose controller is a 4-billion-parameter model running locally on a laptop (with a lighter 2B option). It combines read-only search and document-lookup tools, keyword retrieval with a neural cross-encoder reranker, a deterministic rule-based citation verifier (a Python heuristic gate, not a neural model or second agent) with bounded repair, and pre-model input guardrails.\par\smallskip
\textbf{Evaluated (D2 baseline scope):} the primary purpose of the D2 evaluation is to verify that the end-to-end system and measurement harness built by the team run reliably and produce reproducible baseline metrics across two benchmarks of 50 Vietnamese questions each (HCMUT regulations, labour law), three configurations (no verifier, verifier, verifier and reranker), three random seeds, and two diagnostic baselines (1{,}090 recorded trials per controller); broader experiments and system refinements are scheduled for D3. Strict success requires appropriate action selection (answering vs.\ refusing), a correct and complete answer, and full source support for every claim. Outcomes are scored offline by an LLM judge (reported as a lower bound over all planned trials); on a blind 60-item human audit (30 per benchmark), judge--human agreement on strict success is 96.7\% ($\kappa=0.93$) on HCMUT regulations and 90.0\% ($\kappa=0.80$) on labour law.\par\smallskip
\textbf{Result:} with the 4B controller, the full system reaches 60.7\% (HCMUT regulations) and 56.7\% (labour law) strict success, compared with 28.7\% and 28.0\% with the lighter 2B controller (+32.0 and +28.7 percentage points). Without the rule-based citation verifier, 25.6\% (HCMUT) and 28.0\% (labour law) of answers produced by the 4B controller contain non-verbatim quotes or mismatched clause numbers undetected by the prose-only LLM judge; enabling the verifier reduces unverified citations to 0.0\% and increases citation-audited strict success from 55.3\% to 60.7--64.0\% on HCMUT and from 51.3\% to 52.7--56.7\% on labour law.}}
