\hdr{Riskiest assumption and fallback.} The riskiest assumption is that the gains of the current system hold on unseen questions and on long, vague real questions. The initial system reached only 12--19\%, and the revision was designed on the questions it is scored on. If the gains vanish on the new benchmark, we keep the loop, the verifier, the benchmarks and the pipeline, report the measured numbers as they are, and cut the dense-retrieval ablation, the semantic (NLI) check and further domains.

\hdr{Self-assessment: on track, with one main risk.} The system, the benchmarks, the 60-item judge audit and the evaluation pipeline are complete and reproducible, and the current system answers about 57--61\% of questions strictly. The main remaining risk is that the revision's gain was measured on the same questions used to diagnose the initial system and must be confirmed on unseen questions in the final report.
